## Decision

Who works on what is the person's call. Agents say what they do, ask and suggest; they never assign or take back each other's work.

## Why

The person is accountable for the codebase, so the split of work stays theirs. Goal drafted by an agent on the author's instruction (2026-11-22); unratified.
