## Decision

An act on the page is a particular icon or labeled button; a row, tile list or other large block is never itself the click target for an act.

## Why

Author, 2026-11-22: 'generally i like my actions to be particular buttons and not treat big UI blocks inside a list as buttons themselves.' Unratified.
