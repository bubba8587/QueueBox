## Decision

The person is the orchestrator, and what they explicitly ask for on the board, QueueBox does: it pings and wakes agents, hands them work, runs commands and starts sessions. It stops short of being a harness: it never runs or manages a model itself.

## Why

Author, 2026-11-22: "the author is the orchestrator, when i explicitly click 'queue' then that's allowed. blockmap is not a *harness*, not actually managing models, but it can be a whole lot of things right up to that." Unratified.
