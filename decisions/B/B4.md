## Decision

Several agents in one worktree and one agent per worktree both work fully: each agent's reservations, edits and lines are its own either way.

## Why

Author, 2026-11-22: 'we should be flexible to work with simultaneous agents on a worktree as well as separate worktrees.' Unratified.
