## Decision

By default an agent cannot edit inside a block someone else reserves: it is told who has it and what to do instead.

## Why

A warning after the edit leaves the person to untangle the overlap; refusing it keeps the split of work the person made. Author, 2026-11-22: 'you're authorized to add whatever leaves'; unratified.
