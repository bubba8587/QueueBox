## Decision

An agent with nothing to do is prompted to review the board and suggest a distribution, which reaches the person as something to accept, never as a reassignment.

## Why

The split of work is the person's, so a suggestion waits on them. Author, 2026-11-22, asked that an idle agent be bugged 'to review the state of the board and suggest some task distribution'. Unratified.
